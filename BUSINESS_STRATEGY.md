# Secure File Exchange - SaaS Business Strategy

**Status:** Pre-Launch | **Target:** 50 Paid Clients in 6 Months | **Revenue Model:** Subscription + Usage

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Problem:** Organizations waste $500K-$2M annually on document exchange solutions that are either:
- Over-engineered enterprise platforms (expensive, slow to deploy)
- Undersecured consumer tools (not compliant, liability risk)
- Legacy systems (painful UX, high maintenance)

**Solution:** Secure File Exchange — purpose-built B2B SaaS for organizations that need:
- Secure document exchange (not general file sync)
- Compliance-ready (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR traceable)
- Deployable in hours (not months)
- Clear per-use pricing (not black-box enterprise deals)

**Unique Positioning:** "Slack for secure documents" — simple, powerful, compliant.
