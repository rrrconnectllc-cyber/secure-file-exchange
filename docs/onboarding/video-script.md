# SFX for Legal — video script

November 2026. Speak these words over the still frames in `docs/onboarding/figures`. There is no video file yet. Record one segment at a time, in this order.

The pictures use an example firm, Northline Counsel, and an example matter, Hartwell acquisition for Hartwell Holdings. Say that once in the opening. Do not read a web address out of a picture. Do not type or speak a password.

## Opening

**Still:** none. Title card, or hold frame `01-signup.png` without narrating the form yet.
**Length:** about 20 seconds.

**SAY:** This is the setup for SFX for Legal. You will open a matter, file a document, have someone at the firm approve it, and then ask the client to type their name. The pictures use an example firm, Northline Counsel. Use your own names. Follow the steps in order.

**DO NOT SAY:** that a video file is already attached, that this is DocuSign, or that the product sends email.

## Segment 1 — Create the firm account

**Still:** `figures/01-signup.png`
**Frame:** 01
**Length:** about 30 seconds.

**SHOW:** The heading Get Started. The line that Solo is free for one person and one matter. The organization name. The email. Both password boxes. The terms checkbox. Create Account.

**SAY:** From the front page, choose Open a matter. This screen is Get Started. Type the firm name, your email, and a password of at least 12 characters. Type the password again. Check the terms box. Then choose Create Account. Solo is free for one person and one matter. This screen does not ask for a card. After the account exists, this page points you at the setup guide. Create Account does not open the firm until every field is valid. The next segment shows the three messages people miss.

**DO NOT SAY:** that a card is required, or that the account is created if a field is empty.

---

## Segment 2 — The three checks people skip

**Still:** `figures/02-signup-skipped.png`
**Frame:** 02
**Length:** about 30 seconds.

**SHOW:** The red line under the password. The red line under the confirmation. The red line under the empty terms checkbox.

**SAY:** If a check fails, the firm is not created. This picture shows three messages at once. The password must be at least 12 characters. The two passwords must match. You must agree to the terms and privacy policy. A short password and a mismatched confirmation are separate errors. Fix both, and check the box, then choose Create Account again. The firm name and the email are required too.

**DO NOT SAY:** the password that was typed. The boxes are masked. Do not invent an account being created on this frame.

## Segment 3 — Sign in the next time

**Still:** `figures/03-login.png`
**Frame:** 03
**Length:** about 30 seconds.

**SHOW:** Welcome Back. Email. Password. Remember me. Forgot password. Sign In.

**SAY:** The next time, choose Sign in. Use the same email and password, then choose Sign In. Remember me keeps you signed in on this browser. Forgot password does not send an email. The reset page says password reset is not available yet. Use the password from signup. A person who was invited cannot use this screen until they have opened their invite link. That is a later segment.

**DO NOT SAY:** that a reset email is on the way, or that a pending invite can sign in.

## Segment 7 — File the document

**Still:** `figures/07-matter-file.png`
**Frame:** 07
**Length:** about 30 seconds.
**Hold the camera on the top of the page.** Add a document, and the document row. Do not linger on the lower card. That card is segment 10.

**SHOW:** Choose File. The gray File on this matter button. The row for engagement-letter.txt. Download.

**SAY:** Open the matter. Under Add a document, choose the file, then choose File on this matter. That button stays gray until a file is chosen. The screen refuses a file larger than 6 MB. Filing puts the document on the matter. It does not approve it, and it does not send it to anyone. People stop at the gray button. Choose the file first. The button turns solid after a file is chosen.

**DO NOT SAY:** that the file is approved, or that Request approval has already been pressed on this frame.

**Hold on the document row.** Do not move down to Copy acceptance link.

**SHOW:** Request approval on the engagement-letter.txt row.

**SAY:** On that document row, choose Request approval. That adds a waiting decision on Approvals. It does not email anyone, and it does not send the file to the client. Request approval and Copy acceptance link are different buttons. Leave the lower card alone until the document is approved.

**DO NOT SAY:** that an email was sent to a partner or to the client.

## Segment 9 — Approve it, or send it back

**Still:** `figures/08-approvals.png`
**Frame:** 08
**Length:** about 35 seconds.

**SHOW:** The pending row, with Approve and Reject. Then the older approved row, which has no buttons.

**SAY:** Choose Approvals. A waiting document is marked pending, and it has Approve and Reject. Approve means someone at the firm has released it. Reject leaves it on the matter. It does not record client acceptance. A row that is already decided has no buttons. It cannot be changed from this screen. This picture shows the example file twice: one row still pending, and an older row already approved. Your list shows only the decisions your firm has made. Uploading the file did not approve it. A person chooses Approve or Reject here.

**DO NOT SAY:** that both rows are required, or that the viewer must have two copies of the same file.

## Segment 10 — Copy the acceptance link after approval

**Still:** `figures/07-matter-file.png`
**Frame:** 07, same still again
**Length:** about 35 seconds.
**Hold on the lower card only.**

**SHOW:** Ask someone to accept a document. The sentence that this does not send the document to DocuSign. The document menu. Signer name. Signer email. Copy acceptance link.

**SAY:** Go back to the matter. The lower card asks someone to accept a document. This records a typed name. It does not send the document to DocuSign. Choose the document. Type the signer name and the signer email. Both are required. Then choose Copy acceptance link. The link is copied. It is not mailed. The screen will let you copy a link even if Approvals does not say approved yet. Do not send it until it does. Paste the link into your own message.

**DO NOT SAY:** that DocuSign is connected, that an email goes to the signer, or that copying the link means the client has accepted.

Then they choose Create account and join. If the password is short, or the two boxes differ, the account is not created. After they join, the row changes from Pending to Active, and they can sign in. This picture stops before the button is pressed. The password boxes stay empty.

## Segment 18 — Keep the activity record

**Still:** `figures/10-compliance.png`
**Frame:** 10
**Length:** about 25 seconds.

**SHOW:** The sentence under the title, that this is the firm's activity record and not a certification. Then the dated lines, newest at the top.

**SAY:** Choose Compliance reports. The list shows who opened a matter, filed a document, requested approval, approved or rejected it, invited someone, and recorded an acceptance. The newest lines are at the top. This is the firm's activity record. It is not a certification, and it is not a legal opinion about the document. A missing line means that action was not recorded.

**DO NOT SAY:** SOC, HIPAA, GDPR, certified, audit opinion, or that this page proves the document is valid.

## Segment 19 — Settings, and the switches that are only saved

**Still:** `figures/15-settings.png`
**Frame:** 15
**Length:** about 40 seconds.

**SHOW:** Organization name. Default expiration days. Max downloads per file. Require password protection. Enable two-factor auth. The start of Email notifications.

**SAY:** Choose Settings, then General. The organization name is saved, and it shows at the top of the signed-in pages. The expiration number and the max-downloads number are saved on this form. A newly filed document is still kept for 30 days, and it can still be downloaded 100 times. Changing the numbers does not change that yet. The switches for password protection, two-factor, and email notifications are saved when you choose Save changes. They do not add a download password, a second code at sign-in, or an email. Leave them as they are during setup. Do not tell a client those controls are protecting the file. Do not choose Delete Organization while you are setting up. That removes the firm and the files under it.

**DO NOT SAY:** that two-factor is on, that emails will be sent, that new files ask for a password, or that a different expiration is already in effect.

---

## Segment 20 — Leave billing until you mean to pay

**Still:** `figures/16-billing.png`
**Frame:** 16
**Length:** about 30 seconds.

**SHOW:** The Trial card for Northline Counsel, including the line that no card is on file and Solo stays free. The Solo card at $0 with Use Solo. Starter at $29 with Subscribe. Business at $149 with Subscribe. Enterprise, partly visible, at Custom.

**SAY:** Choose Settings, then Billing. This example firm is still on Trial, which is why you see Use Solo. A new firm starts on Solo. Solo is free for one person and one open matter. No card is on file. Use Solo moves a trial firm onto that free plan. Starter is 29 dollars a month for 3 people. Business is 149 dollars a month for 15 people. Enterprise is arranged from Contact us. After a firm subscribes, this same page shows Cancel subscription. Canceling stops the next charge and moves the firm back to Solo. Documents already filed stay. These buttons are available because this sign-in is an owner. A member sees them gray. You do not need a card to open the first matter.

**DO NOT SAY:** that Cancel subscription is on this still, a card number, that the viewer is being charged, or that a Member can subscribe.

**Still:** none, or hold frame `09-signature-requests.png`.
**Length:** about 25 seconds.

**SAY:** You are done when you can sign in, a matter has both names, a document is filed, Approvals shows it as approved, you sent the acceptance link yourself, the client sees the words "Your acceptance is recorded," and anyone you invited shows Active rather than Pending. If a numbered step does not match your screen, email hello@securefileexchange.com with the step number. Do not book a call for a short password, a missing client name, a gray File button, or a link you have not copied yet.

**DO NOT SAY:** that someone will call them, or that the product emails the client for them.
